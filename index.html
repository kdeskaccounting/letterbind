Letterbind — a free daily word-chain puzzle

Letterbind is a free daily word game: a word-chain roguelike played on a Boggle-style grid.

To play, drag a path through touching letters to spell a word. Beat the round's target score before the ink runs out. Clear the round, pick an upgrade that bends the rules in your favour, and carry on until a round beats you.

Everyone gets the same daily puzzle, a timed Blitz board and unlimited free play, with a leaderboard for you and your friends. No accounts, no downloads, no cost — it runs in a phone browser at letterbind.com.
